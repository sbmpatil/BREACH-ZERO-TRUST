BREACH: HUNT THE INTRUDER

ASYMMETRIC CYBER DEDUCTION

Find the signal.
Hunt the intruder.

One hidden attacker. A SOC drowning in noisy alerts. Twelve rounds before the Crown Jewels disappear.

HOW TO PLAY

Intruder: secretly enter through VPN, Web, or Email. Each 45-second turn, Stealth Move one hop (only the zone leaks), Exploit Chain up to two hops (exact destination leaks; 3 uses), Plant Decoy (fake HIGH alert within 3 hops; 2 uses), or Lie Low (no alert; never twice consecutively).

SOC: after the attacker's move, a 60-second SIEM feed mixes the real signal with 2–4 benign alerts. Investigate a node to learn whether and when it was visited; Isolate it to cut it off forever (3 total, instant capture if occupied); or deploy up to two active honeypots.

Win: the attacker reaches CROWN JEWELS and survives the following SOC phase. The SOC wins by isolating the attacker, trapping them, or holding for 12 rounds.

EXAMPLE TURN
Intruder: WS-02 → Mail Server by Stealth Move.
Feed: [LOW] Suspicious lateral activity — Zone: Server Zone + noise.
SOC deduces likely routes, investigates Mail Server, and adds that visit to the trail.
